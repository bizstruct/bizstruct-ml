## Рубрика оцінювання

Шкала 1–5 по кожному критерію. Оцінюється **проєкт цілком**, не окремий блок.

### K5. Придатність гіпотез до перевірки

- **1** — гіпотези без критерію перевірки («клієнтам потрібен наш продукт»)
- **3** — конкретні, але спосіб перевірки неочевидний або результат неоднозначний
- **5** — кожна гіпотеза містить перевірюване твердження, зрозумілий експеримент, покрито всі три категорії ризику

### K2. Специфічність

Чи прив'язаний зміст до цієї конкретної ідеї. Робочий тест: підставити іншу ідею з тієї ж галузі — якщо текст лишається доречним, специфічність низька.

- **1** — переважно шаблонні формулювання, ідею можна замінити без шкоди
- **3** — частина змісту прив'язана до ідеї, частина — загальні місця
- **5** — конкретика в усіх артефактах: названі ролі, процеси, обмеження галузі

### K4. Економічна правдоподібність

Чи тримається бізнес-логіка. Дивитись: чи є хтось, хто платить; чи співвідносяться витрати й надходження; чи відповідає канал збуту сегменту; чи не суперечить обраний патерн решті моделі.

- **1** — модель не працює економічно
- **3** — життєздатна, але є непроговорене сумнівне припущення
- **5** — економіка узгоджена, ризиковані припущення винесені в гіпотези

### K3. Змістовна повнота

Чи розкрито кожен артефакт по суті. Оцінюється не наявність полів (її гарантує схема), а чи несе кожен пункт інформацію.

- **1** — формальне заповнення, пункти-заглушки, дублювання змісту між блоками
- **3** — основні блоки розкрито, окремі поверхові або повторюють сусідні
- **5** — кожен артефакт несе власний зміст, жоден не є переказом іншого

### K1. Узгодженість між артефактами

Чи описують усі артефакти одну й ту саму бізнес-модель. Дивитись: сегмент у карті емпатії ↔ персона в сценарії ↔ customer_segments у канвасі; обрана монетизація ↔ revenue_streams; болі клієнта ↔ ціннісні пропозиції; ключові ресурси ↔ структура витрат; гіпотези ↔ припущення канвасу.

- **1** — артефакти описують фактично різні бізнеси
- **3** — загальна лінія витримана, є одна-дві помітні розбіжності в деталях
- **5** — наскрізна узгодженість, кожен артефакт виводиться з попередніх

### Що НЕ впливає на оцінку

- обсяг тексту (довший артефакт не є кращим)
- впевненість тону
- форматування, структурованість
- граматика й стилістика
- кількість пунктів у блоці (п'ять поверхових гірші за три змістовні)

## Формат відповіді

По кожному з п'яти критеріїв, у порядку, наведеному вище:

1. **Обґрунтування** (2–3 речення) — чому саме такий бал. Це йде ПЕРЕД балом: спершу поясни, потім став оцінку, а не навпаки.
2. **Бал** — ціле число від 1 до 5.
3. **Цитата** — коротка цитата з матеріалу нижче, що підтверджує бал.

Сумарний бал не виводити — жодного середнього чи підсумкового числа по всіх п'яти критеріях.

## Блок: Карта емпатії

**Каже**
- As a computer science student, I need practical tasks that let me practice the exact skills employers expect.
- I do not have time for a large project when I only want to improve one weak skill.
- It is hard to tell whether an online exercise is appropriate for my current level.
- I want practice that relates to real professional work, not only theoretical coursework.

**Думає**
- I need evidence that I can apply what I learn, not just say that I completed a course.
- If I choose tasks that are too difficult, I will waste time and lose motivation.
- A short, clearly scoped assignment would fit between classes, exams, and other responsibilities.
- Tasks created by working practitioners or instructors should be more relevant than random exercises I find online.

**Робить**
- The computer science student searches across learning sites, repositories, and social media for coding exercises and mini-project ideas.
- The computer science student filters opportunities informally by topic, such as programming, databases, or testing, and estimates difficulty from limited descriptions.
- The computer science student starts tutorials or projects but abandons them when their scope exceeds the available time.
- The computer science student asks classmates, instructors, and online communities for recommendations on what to practice next.

**Відчуває**
- The computer science student feels uncertain about which practical task will genuinely build a needed professional skill.
- The computer science student feels frustrated when available exercises are either overly basic or too advanced.
- The computer science student feels pressured to gain practical experience before applying for internships or entry-level roles.
- The computer science student feels motivated when a small task has a clear outcome and a realistic time commitment.

**Болі**
- The computer science student struggles to find short practice assignments focused on one specific professional skill.
- The computer science student cannot reliably compare tasks by difficulty, specialization, and time required before starting.
- The computer science student encounters scattered learning materials that lack realistic context from educators or industry practitioners.
- The computer science student loses time selecting, scoping, and validating suitable practice instead of practicing.
- The computer science student finds that long projects are difficult to complete alongside academic deadlines.

**Вигоди**
- The computer science student wants to quickly find a practical task matched to a chosen skill, such as SQL querying, API testing, or version control.
- The computer science student wants clear difficulty labels so that each assignment is challenging but achievable.
- The computer science student wants an accurate time estimate to fit practice into an available study session.
- The computer science student wants assignments organized by specialization to create a focused path toward a target role.
- The computer science student wants confidence that completed tasks reflect practical expectations from instructors and working professionals.

## Блок: Варіанти бізнес-моделі

### Subscription · SkillSprint
- Аудиторія: Computer science students preparing for internships or entry-level roles who need focused practice between classes and exams.
- Ціннісна пропозиція: Get a personalized stream of short, practitioner-reviewed tasks matched to your target skill, level, and available study time.
- Опис: Students pay a monthly subscription for unlimited access to curated micro-assignments, skill paths, difficulty matching, and completion evidence. It directly addresses pain #1 (finding short assignments for a specific professional skill), pain #2 (unreliable difficulty and time comparisons), and pain #5 (long projects do not fit academic schedules). It also delivers gains #1–#5 through filters, credible task authors, time estimates, and role-oriented practice paths.
- Монетизація: subscription
- Ключова метрика: Monthly recurring revenue (MRR)
- Час до цінності: 15 minutes to select and start a matched task
- Оцінка: 88 — Strong fit because repeat practice across multiple skills creates recurring use, while the core student need is rapid, dependable task discovery. The main constraint is student price sensitivity, so a useful free tier or campus discount may be needed to reduce churn.

### Transaction Fee · SkillTask Marketplace
- Аудиторія: Computer science students seeking a single verified practical task for an immediate skill gap, such as SQL, API testing, or Git.
- Ціннісна пропозиція: Buy only the short, expert-created challenge you need, with a clear difficulty level, time estimate, and practical outcome.
- Опис: The platform charges students per task or challenge pack and retains a transaction fee when educators and practitioners sell their assignments. This model addresses pain #3 (scattered materials without realistic context), pain #4 (time lost selecting and validating practice), and gains #1–#3 by making each paid task clearly skill-tagged, level-rated, and time-boxed. It fits students who need targeted help before an interview, class deadline, or internship application rather than ongoing access.
- Монетизація: transaction_fee
- Ключова метрика: Gross merchandise value (GMV)
- Час до цінності: 10 minutes to purchase and begin a task
- Оцінка: 72 — The pay-per-task format aligns well with students' short, specific practice needs and lowers the commitment barrier. It is weaker than subscription because low ticket sizes require high transaction volume and the marketplace needs a reliable supply of quality creators.

### Retainer Plus SaaS · SkillSprint Campus
- Аудиторія: Computer science students at universities or coding academies that want structured, job-relevant micro-practice alongside coursework.
- Ціннісна пропозиція: Give students an always-current library of short, level-appropriate professional tasks through institution-sponsored access.
- Опис: Universities or coding academies pay an annual SaaS license plus a content-curation retainer, providing enrolled computer science students with institution-branded access to practitioner and instructor-created assignments. The service targets pain #3 (learning materials lack realistic educator or practitioner context) and pain #4 (students lose time choosing and validating practice), while supporting gains #4 and #5 through specialization paths and credible assignments. Institutional access removes much of the individual student's price barrier while keeping the student persona as the end user
- Монетизація: retainer_plus_saas
- Ключова метрика: Annual contract value (ACV)
- Час до цінності: 1 week from institutional onboarding to a student's first matched task
- Оцінка: 81 — Strong because institutions can fund access for price-sensitive students and value structured employability practice, while the content retainer supports quality control. Sales cycles and procurement requirements make customer acquisition slower than a direct-to-student subscription.

## Блок: Архітектура моделі

**Епіцентр:** offer_driven

The primary differentiation is concentrated in the value_propositions section: students can locate narrowly scoped, job-relevant assignments for an exact skill and select them using credible difficulty, specialization, and time labels. The key_resources, activities, channels, and relationships are organized to deliver and substantiate this offer, particularly practitioner-reviewed content, matching, and completion evidence. The mixed subscription, transaction, and institutional revenues support delivery but are not themselves the central innovation.

**Патерн:** multi_sided_platform

The canvas structures the service as an intermediary between students seeking targeted practice and a supply side of educators and working practitioners who create, review, and, in the per-task model, sell assignments. Key_partners and key_activities explicitly establish creator submission, review, pricing, and revenue-sharing workflows, while student-facing web, campus, and community channels drive demand. Institutional buyers add a further sponsoring/access side, but the core interdependence is between quality task creators and students who discover and complete those tasks.

## Блок: Гіпотези

- **[desirability / q1]** At least 40% of 100 computer science students who are preparing for internships will start one recommended 15-90 minute task within 7 days after filtering by a named skill, self-assessed level, and available time; a start rate below 40% falsifies demand for the core matched-task value proposition.
- **[feasibility / q1]** At least 60% of students who complete a task will rate its displayed difficulty and time estimate as accurate (4 or 5 out of 5) across the first 100 task completions; a rating below 60% falsifies the assumption that the structured assignment library can enable confident task selection.
- **[viability / q1]** At least 15% of students who complete two free tasks in a 14-day trial will purchase a $7-10 monthly subscription for unlimited access within 30 days; conversion below 15% falsifies the viability of the student subscription revenue stream at this price range.
- **[feasibility / q1]** At least 10 verified educators or practitioners will each submit 5 publishable micro-assignments within 45 days when offered editorial support and revenue share, yielding at least 50 assignments that pass quality review; fewer than 50 approved assignments falsifies the ability to establish the creator supply required by the multi-sided-platform architecture.
- **[desirability / q2]** At least 50% of students who start a task will finish it within 150% of its stated time estimate, measured across 100 starts; a completion rate below 50% falsifies the assumption that short, clearly scoped assignments fit students’ schedules well enough to support repeat use.
- **[viability / q3]** At least 3 of 20 contacted universities or coding academies will agree to a paid pilot or sign a letter of intent for institution-sponsored access priced at a minimum of $5,000 per year within 90 days; fewer than 3 commitments falsifies near-term viability of the institutional SaaS revenue stream.
